# Features

What Realm does today.

## World
- 25 maps in five places: Petal Isle (Petalbrook, Sprout Meadow, Puddle
  Path, Breezy Bluff, Petal Pier), the Saltwhistle coast (Docks, Market,
  Gull Cliffs, Tidepool Shore, Lighthouse Hill), Fernwood (Fernwood Trail,
  the tree-top village Mossbough, Canopy Walk, Glowcap Hollow, Thornbark's
  Grove), Windcrest Pass (Foothills, Scout's Camp, Cliffside Climb,
  Frostbite Ledge, Summit Shrine) and the Undercroft below the Market.
- Portals (stand in one and press Up), Captain Marlo's ship between Petal
  Pier and Saltwhistle with a voyage scene, a gate into the Undercroft
  opened by the story.
- Footholds, ropes, ladders, rope bridges, ramps, one-way platforms; a
  harbor sea under the piers at the Docks and Petal Pier.
- A day and night clock that tints the world; region skies and parallax.
- World map window with visited maps, the route and where you are; a
  minimap in three sizes.

## Hero
- Character creator (typed name, hair style and color, eyes, skin), three
  character slots, autosave on every map change.
- Chibi avatar on one construction for hero, townsfolk and gear; poses for
  walking, jumping, climbing, prone, swings, casts, hurt and cheer.
- Four core stats, five ability points per level with auto-assign and a
  preview of every change; HP, MP, attack, magic, defense, critical, Speed
  and Jump (Speed and Jump change movement).
- Hyper jump (forward or straight up), prone, drop-through.
- Beginner to warrior or mage at level 10 through a job quest; three
  beginner skills and eight per job (strikes, cleave, quake, rush, buffs,
  bolts, chain lightning, starfall, heal), learned with skill points.
- Inventory in equip, use and etc tabs; six equipment slots with level and
  job requirements and random rolls by rarity; item cards comparing with
  what is worn.
- Shops in every town, buying in quantity and selling.
- Key binding window: an on-screen keyboard with a palette of learned
  skills, items and actions; place by key or mouse drag; per character.

## Monsters and bosses
- 14 monster kinds over ten archetypes, spawn points with a population cap
  and respawn, passive and aggressive kinds, elites.
- Three area bosses with phases on longer respawn timers: Thornbark,
  Frostmane and Murkmaw (the last).
- Drops picked up with a key: coins, potions, monster drops, quest items,
  equipment.

## Story
- 16 townsfolk with conversations, quest markers, 26 quests (kill, collect,
  talk, deliver, boss) in chains, a quest log and a tracker; the story ends
  when Murkmaw falls, with an ending screen and credits; play continues.

## Presentation
- HUD: level badge, HP/MP gauges with damage trails, experience, quick
  slots with cooldown sweeps, buffs, clock, quest tracker, message log,
  boss bar, map title.
- Damage numbers that stack, hit sparks, skill effects, level-up pillar.
- Title with the demo bot playing behind the menu, pause and settings,
  fainting and waking in town.
- Synthesized region music with night takes, boss and voyage themes, and
  effects for every action.
